## Overview

This repository documents my learning journey throughout the Digital Signal Processing course. It contains theoretical concepts, mathematical analysis, programming exercises, laboratory practices, and practical implementations.

The primary objective is to develop a strong understanding of digital signal processing techniques and explore their applications in mechatronics, robotics, embedded systems, and intelligent engineering systems.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand continuous-time and discrete-time signals.
- Study the mathematical foundations of digital signal processing.
- Analyze discrete-time systems and their properties.
- Understand convolution and correlation.
- Explore Fourier analysis and frequency-domain representation.
- Study the Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) and Fast Fourier Transform (FFT).
- Understand digital filter design and implementation.
- Apply DSP techniques to practical engineering problems.
